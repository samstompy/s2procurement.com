The Problem

Most companies are overpaying suppliers and have no easy way to see it.

Purchasing costs quietly creep upward — through outdated contracts, unreviewed invoices, and vendors who know a renegotiation call is unlikely. Nobody inside the business owns catching it.

01 — CONTRACTS

Stale supplier terms

Agreements signed years ago, never revisited, sitting well above current market rates.

02 — VISIBILITY

No one is auditing spend

Invoices get paid, not reviewed. Small overcharges compound quietly across a full purchasing calendar.

03 — LEVERAGE

Nobody wants the negotiation

Renegotiating with an existing vendor is uncomfortable internally — so it simply doesn't happen.

Services

A focused set of services built around one outcome: lower cost, same or better supply.

01

Procurement Analysis

A full review of your current purchasing structure — vendors, categories, contract terms, and where spend is concentrated — to identify where cost reduction is realistically available.

02

Invoice Review

Line-by-line examination of supplier invoices to catch pricing drift, unauthorized fee increases, and billing errors that go unnoticed in normal accounts-payable workflows.

03

Supplier Negotiation

Direct negotiation with your suppliers' commercial teams on pricing, terms, and volume structures — handled on your behalf, without disrupting the relationship.

04

Vendor Management

Ongoing oversight of key supplier relationships so pricing gains are enforced and don't quietly erode over the following contract cycles.

05

Cost Optimization

Structural recommendations — consolidating vendors, adjusting order volumes, or resequencing purchasing — that reduce cost beyond what negotiation alone can achieve.
